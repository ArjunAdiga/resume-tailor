Frontend Engineer - Northstar Labs (Remote)

About the role
Northstar Labs builds tooling for small clinical teams. We are looking for a frontend engineer who cares about clarity, accessibility, and fast interfaces. You will work with designers and product managers to ship customer-facing features.

What you will do
- Build and maintain customer-facing features in React and TypeScript.
- Turn Figma designs into responsive, accessible interfaces with modern CSS.
- Work in our Next.js application and help improve page load performance.
- Write focused unit tests and participate in code review.

What we are looking for
- 2+ years of professional frontend experience.
- Strong React, TypeScript, HTML, and CSS skills.
- Experience with Next.js and component-based design.
- Familiarity with accessibility standards and responsive layouts.
- Comfort writing unit tests with Vitest or a similar framework.
- Clear written communication and attention to detail.

Nice to have
- Experience with GraphQL APIs and Apollo Client.
- Familiarity with Redux for state management.
- Node.js service experience and basic Docker knowledge.
- Exposure to AWS and CI/CD pipelines.
- Experience with Playwright for end-to-end testing.

We are an equal opportunity employer. Applicants from all backgrounds are welcome.
